Sounds and sources

The music is played live in your browser, different for every year and for every life. Around it are real recordings of the world, and beneath each age, a fact from a public source.

When a life ends

The length of each life is drawn once, at birth, from the World Health Organization's 2019 global life table for both sexes (Global Health Observatory), extended past 85 along a Gompertz curve fitted to WHO's life expectancy at 85. It is kept sealed on your device. The music never knows it.
